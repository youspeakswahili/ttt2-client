# TTT2 Client 0.5.0-alpha

The first alpha on the new native game build (our own recompilation and rollback netcode).

Big one. Online matches now play out through the client start to finish, and voice chat is in.

**Matches**
- When a match is found you get 10 seconds to accept it. Press A / Cross on your pad, hit Enter, or click. B / Circle or Esc declines. If you're in practice when one pops, the prompt shows up in the game itself, so you don't have to alt-tab.
- When a match is found the game opens straight into character select with your team already picked. Stage is random. You don't touch anything until the fight starts.
- Before the match you can see where your opponent is and whether they're on wired or Wi-Fi, with signal bars.
- You never have to tell the client who won. The game reports it when you close it. Play as many matches as you like in one sitting; whoever won more takes the result. (TODO before release: check in real matches, Test 33.)
- Your MMR and theirs sit under the health bars during the fight. You can turn that off in Settings if you'd rather not see it.

**Voice chat**
- Push to talk, open mic, or off. Push to talk is on Caps Lock by default, but you can bind it to any key or mouse side button.
- On open mic you can bind a mute key. MUTED shows in the corner while you're muted, so you won't forget.
- Pick which mic you talk into and where you hear your opponent.
- Volume and device changes apply mid-match, so there's no need to wait for the next game.
- A small MIC marker shows in the corner while you're transmitting.

**Practice**
- Practice is on the PLAY page now: your team against whoever you want, on any stage, straight into the fight.
- While you're searching online there's a one-click practice button. It closes on its own when a match is found.
